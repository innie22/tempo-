    What is Tempo?
    
                
    
                
            
    
            
    
                
    
                    Tempo is a calm, supportive, non-clinical student wellbeing platform built to help university students manage academic pressure, break deadline paralysis, and find support when university life feels heavy.
                
    
                
    
                    
    Our Core Commitments:
    
                    
      
                        
    • No Toxic Productivity: Missing a habit or study block is never treated as a personal failure.
      • 
                        
    • The ONE Next Action: When panic strikes, we help deconstruct chaos into a single 25-minute doable step.
      • 
                        
    • Safety First: 24/7 crisis lifelines (988 & 741741) are always 1 click away without any quizzes or gates.
      • 
                    
    
                
    
                
    
                    Use Tempo to decompress, track your gentle habits, run quiet focus blocks, and access peer encouragement whenever you need a mindful anchor.
